HEARTHSTONE DECK PREDICTOR

A WPF plugin for Hearthstone Deck Tracker that predicts an opponent's likely deck in real time.

Purpose:
Returning Hearthstone players can find it difficult to keep up with a changing meta and recognise opposing archetypes. The plugin uses cards revealed during a match to progressively estimate the opponent's likely deck and strategy.

Architecture and behaviour:
- Integrates with Hearthstone Deck Tracker's internal API.
- Reacts to live game and opponent card-play events.
- Retrieves current Standard meta deck data from HSReplay.net at the start of a game.
- Stores deck information in memory for fast matching.
- Uses hash-based lookups and progressive filtering to eliminate incompatible decks as cards are revealed.
- Ranks remaining candidates to identify likely archetypes.
- Displays likely remaining cards.
- Displays predicted deck/archetype information.
- Surfaces matchup odds against the player's likely archetype.

The goal is to help players identify threats, understand the current meta, and make better-informed decisions during a match.
